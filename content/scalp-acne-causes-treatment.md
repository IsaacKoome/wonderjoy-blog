## ðŸ§ª Do Not Mix Random Acne Treatments

Salicylic acid can help clogged pores on accessible hairline skin. Benzoyl peroxide may help inflammatory acne but can irritate and bleach towels, clothing and hair. Leave-on facial products are difficult to distribute through hair and may not be designed for the scalp.

A pharmacist or dermatologist can recommend a scalp-appropriate wash or medicine after identifying the condition. Avoid steroid creams, antibiotics and antifungals unless they match the diagnosis.

## ðŸš« Please Do Not Pop Scalp Bumps

Hair makes the area difficult to see and clean. Picking can increase inflammation, infection, dark marks and scarring. Use a warm compress for comfort while arranging care if a bump is tender.

## ðŸš© When to See a Dermatologist

Get help for persistent or recurring bumps, significant pain, fever, spreading redness, pus-filled clusters, scarring or hair loss. Seek earlier care if you are immunocompromised. A clinician may need to examine or culture a lesion before deciding between acne, bacterial folliculitis, yeast-related folliculitis or another condition.

## â“ Frequently Asked Questions

### Can dry shampoo cause scalp bumps?

It can contribute to buildup if it repeatedly replaces washing. It absorbs oil but does not remove dead cells or microorganisms.

### Does an oily scalp mean poor hygiene?

No. Oil production varies. The goal is a wash schedule that prevents uncomfortable buildup without damaging the hair.

### Will facial acne products work on the scalp?

Not automatically. The formulation, hair exposure and actual diagnosis matter.
