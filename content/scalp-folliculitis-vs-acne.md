Acne and folliculitis can both produce pimple-like scalp bumps. Acne begins with clogged pores and often includes different lesion types. Folliculitis means a hair follicle is inflamed or infected and may appear as a sudden crop of similar bumps, sometimes itchy or tender.

Appearance overlaps enough that a photo or home checklist cannot confirm the diagnosis.

Acne may include blackheads, whiteheads and larger inflamed spots together. Bumps may extend onto the forehead, hairline or neck and follow use of oily styling products. Improvement can take four to six weeks after a clogging product is removed.

Folliculitis often centers around individual hairs. Spots may look quite similar to one another and can itch, hurt or contain pus. Friction, shaving, occlusion and microorganisms are possible contributors. Yeast-related folliculitis and bacterial folliculitis do not use the same treatment.
