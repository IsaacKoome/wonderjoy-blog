Mild puffiness after waking is common. Fluid can collect around the loose tissue beneath the eyes overnight, especially after salty food, alcohol, crying, poor sleep or lying flat. Allergies and irritated eyelids can add swelling. It usually improves as you get upright and move through the morning.

Puffiness is different from sudden severe eyelid swelling. Eye pain, vision changes, fever, spreading redness, breathing difficulty or swelling of the lips or tongue needs urgent care.

## 🧊 Use a Cool, Clean Compress

Rest a cool—not frozen—clean washcloth over closed eyes for several minutes. Cold temporarily narrows vessels and may reduce swelling. Do not press ice directly on thin eyelid skin, and do not use raw food, fragranced tea bags or shared masks near the eyes.

Wash reusable masks after each use and stop if the skin becomes numb or painful.

## 🛏️ Adjust the Overnight Triggers

If puffiness is frequent, compare ordinary nights with evenings involving more salt, alcohol, crying or very little sleep. Slight head elevation may help some people, provided it is comfortable and medically appropriate. Do not chase a “detox”; kidneys and liver already regulate fluid balance.

## 🫧 Calm Itchy, Irritated Skin

Rubbing from allergies can worsen both swelling and dark circles. Use a mild fragrance-free cleanser on the face, keep it out of the eye and ask a clinician about persistent nasal or eye allergy symptoms.

## ☀️ Protect Instead of Scrubbing

Repeated rubbing and unprotected sun can make the eye area look darker over time. Apply tolerated broad-spectrum SPF 30+ carefully around intact skin, and use sunglasses and a hat outdoors.

## 🩺 When to Arrange an Appointment

Seek advice if swelling is persistent, one-sided, painful, accompanied by a rash or linked with swelling elsewhere. Thyroid, kidney and other medical conditions can sometimes contribute, but puffiness alone cannot diagnose them.

Compare mornings under similar lighting before deciding a remedy worked; normal day-to-day fluid shifts can otherwise be misleading.

## ❓ Quick Questions

**Do eye rollers drain puffiness?** Cooling and gentle contact may give a temporary effect; pressure should remain very light.

**Will drinking more water fix it?** Normal hydration is sensible, but excessive water is not a treatment.

**Why is one eye puffier?** Sleep position can contribute, but persistent one-sided swelling needs assessment.

## ✅ Bottom Line

For ordinary morning puffiness, use a cool clean compress, reduce identifiable triggers and avoid rubbing. A lasting bag or medically significant swelling needs a different solution.
