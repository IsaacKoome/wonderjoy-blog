A painful spot beside the mouth is easy to call a cold sore, but not every crack is herpes. **Angular cheilitis** usually affects the corner where the lips meet. A **cold sore** more often begins with tingling or burning followed by grouped fluid-filled blisters on or near the lip.

Appearance alone is not always enough, especially after scratching, crusting or applying several products.

## 📍 Angular Cheilitis Favors the Corners

Common clues include a fissure that hurts when opening the mouth, soggy or scaly skin, bleeding and crusting at one or both corners. Saliva pooling in the fold can start irritant dermatitis. Yeast or bacteria may then contribute.

Dentures, deep mouth folds, lip licking, dry skin, diabetes and some nutritional deficiencies can increase risk. That does not mean every case needs a supplement or antifungal treatment.

## 🫧 Cold Sores Often Form Grouped Blisters

Cold sores are caused by herpes simplex virus and can recur. Tingling, itching or burning may come before small grouped blisters that break and crust. They can spread through direct contact, especially while lesions are active.

Do not share lip products, cups, towels or razors during a suspected outbreak. Wash hands after touching the area and avoid picking.

## 🧴 Safe Care While You Are Unsure

Keep the area clean, dry it gently and stop licking. A bland barrier ointment can protect a simple saliva-related corner crack. Avoid acids, scrubs, essential oils and squeezing. Do not apply a leftover steroid, antibiotic or antifungal without knowing the cause; steroids can worsen some infections.

A fragrance-free moisturizer can support intact surrounding skin, not an open or weeping lesion.

## 🩺 When Diagnosis Changes Treatment

Early antiviral treatment can shorten some cold-sore outbreaks, while angular cheilitis may require an antifungal, antibacterial or anti-inflammatory treatment depending on the cause. Contact a pharmacist or clinician early if you suspect a first cold sore, are immunocompromised, are pregnant, or have frequent or severe recurrences.

Protect the face and lips from strong sun because UV exposure can trigger cold sores in some people.

## 🚨 Seek Prompt Help For

Get medical care for eye pain or lesions near the eye, severe swelling, fever, rapidly spreading redness, pus, trouble eating or drinking, or a lesion that does not heal. Babies, people with weakened immunity and those with severe eczema may need earlier advice for suspected herpes.

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions

### Can angular cheilitis spread to someone else?

The irritated fold itself is not necessarily contagious. Associated yeast or bacteria can be present, but it is not managed like a herpes outbreak.

### Can a cold sore occur at the mouth corner?

Yes. Location is a clue, not proof. Blister pattern, recurrence and testing may help.

### Should I take vitamins for cracked corners?

Not automatically. Recurrent disease deserves assessment before supplements.

## ✅ Bottom Line

Fissures at the corner favor angular cheilitis; grouped recurrent blisters favor a cold sore. When uncertain, protect the skin and get the diagnosis before choosing medicated cream.
