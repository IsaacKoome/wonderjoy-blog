Physical methods include salt or sugar scrubs, combs and silicone scalp brushes. They can dislodge visible residue, but pressure is difficult to control through dense hair. Grainy scrubs may remain behind, and hard tools can injure follicles.

Salicylic acid loosens scale and helps clear oil from pores. It appears in some dandruff and scalp products. Use a scalp-labeled formula as directed; a facial leave-on liquid should not be poured throughout hair unless its label says it is suitable.

## ðŸš« Who Should Skip It?

Avoid scalp exfoliation over open skin, infection, active eczema or psoriasis plaques, recent chemical burns, fresh surgical sites or unexplained hair loss. Ask a clinician before combining it with prescription scalp medicine.

## â“ Frequently Asked Questions

### Does scalp exfoliation make hair grow?

There is no good evidence that a scrub creates new hair follicles or treats medical hair loss.

### Can I use a face exfoliant on my scalp?

Not automatically. Scalp formulations account for hair, rinsing and distribution.

### Is tingling normal?

Do not use sensation as a success measure. Burning or lasting discomfort means rinse and stop.
