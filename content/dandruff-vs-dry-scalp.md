White flakes on a dark shirt can look like an obvious case of “dry scalp,” but flaking has several possible causes. Dandruff is often connected with an oily, irritated scalp and may sit on the mild end of seborrheic dermatitis. Simple dryness is more likely after harsh washing, cold weather, low humidity or products that strip the scalp.

You cannot diagnose the cause from flake color alone. The pattern, oiliness, itch, rash and response to a careful routine provide better clues.

## 🔎 Compare the Clues

**A dry scalp** may feel tight and show small, loose flakes. The hair may also feel dry, especially after frequent hot showers or a strong shampoo. There is usually little greasy scale.

**Dandruff** can produce white or gray flakes with itch. The scalp may be normal-to-oily rather than dry. When redness, greasy scale or flaking also appears around the eyebrows, ears or sides of the nose, seborrheic dermatitis becomes more likely and deserves a clinician’s review.

## 🫧 Start With a Gentle Reset

For one to two weeks, avoid scratching, scalp brushes, fragranced oils and several new treatments at once. Wash with lukewarm water, apply shampoo mainly to the scalp and rinse thoroughly. Clean the skin at the hairline and behind the ears without scrubbing.

## 🧴 When an Anti-Dandruff Shampoo Makes Sense

If regular washing does not settle oily, itchy flakes, look for an anti-dandruff shampoo containing an active such as ketoconazole, selenium sulfide, zinc pyrithione or salicylic acid. Follow the bottle exactly; some products need several minutes of contact before rinsing.

Apply medicated shampoo to the **scalp**, not automatically through dry lengths. Curly, coily, bleached or fragile hair may need less frequent use and conditioner on the lengths afterward. If one active fails after a fair trial, a pharmacist or dermatologist can help you choose another.

## 💧 What Helps Simple Dryness

Space out unnecessary washes, use a mild shampoo, lower the water temperature and condition according to your hair type. If the outer hairline or skin behind the ears is dry, a small amount of fragrance-free moisturizer can help that exposed skin. Thick oils across a flaky scalp may trap residue or worsen some conditions, so “more oil” is not a universal answer.

## 🚩 Do Not Self-Treat Every Flaky Scalp

Book medical care if the scalp is painful, swollen, weeping, crusted, causing patchy hair loss or not improving after about a month of correctly used anti-dandruff shampoo. Psoriasis, eczema, contact dermatitis, fungal infection and other conditions can resemble dandruff.

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions

### Is dandruff caused by poor hygiene?

No. Irregular washing can make flakes more visible, but dandruff is not proof that someone is unclean and it is not contagious.

### Can I scrape flakes away?

Avoid nails and harsh scalp tools. Scratching can injure follicles and increase inflammation.

### Should I stop all hair oils?

Pause products touching the scalp while troubleshooting. You may still use a tolerated conditioner or oil on hair lengths if it does not reach the scalp or hairline.

## ✅ Bottom Line

Small loose flakes with tightness suggest dryness; persistent itchy flakes on an oilier or inflamed scalp fit dandruff more closely. Begin gently, use medicated shampoo as directed when appropriate and seek care for severe or stubborn symptoms.
