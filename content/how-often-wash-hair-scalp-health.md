The American Academy of Dermatology advises washing based on how often hair gets dirty or oily. A straight, oily scalp may need daily or near-daily shampoo. ## ðŸƒ Adjust for Sweat and Styling

After a sweaty workout, rinse or wash when practical and clean headwear. If a protective style limits full washing, keep tension comfortable, clean accessible scalp carefully and follow advice suited to the style. Product layering is not a substitute for cleansing.

Dry shampoo is a short-term oil absorber. AAD dermatologists recommend regular shampoo and water after one or two dry-shampoo uses because it does not actually clean the scalp.

## ðŸš© When Frequency Is Not the Real Problem

Persistent itch, pain, pus, thick scale, sudden shedding or patchy hair loss is not solved by endlessly changing wash days. See a dermatologist to check for seborrheic dermatitis, psoriasis, contact dermatitis, infection or hair-loss disease.

## â“ Frequently Asked Questions

### Is daily washing harmful?

Not inherently. It can suit an oily scalp when the shampoo and technique are gentle enough.

### Is washing once a week unhygienic?

No. It may suit some textures and routines. Symptoms and residue matter more than judgment.

### Should I shampoo twice?

Only when one pass does not remove substantial oil or styling product. It is not always necessary.
